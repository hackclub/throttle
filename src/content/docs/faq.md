## Who can join?

Teens 13–18, anywhere in the world. You need to be under 19 when you submit. One Hack Club account per person.

## Do I need experience?

No. Lots of people start with a foamboard glider. Pick Tier 1, log what you learn, and ask in #throttle when you get stuck.

## How much can I get?

There's no cap. Your grant is your approved design hours × your tier rate, so 12 h at Tier 2 is $60.00. See [Tier rates](/docs/tiers).

## Who picks my tier?

You do, when you create your aircraft. Reviewers can move it up or down at Design Review if it doesn't match the build.

## What do I need to ship?

A public repo, a `bom.csv` in the repo root, a journal of your hours, and a video of the maiden flight.

## What counts as an aircraft?

If it flies, it counts: drones, RC planes, gliders, helicopters, low-power rockets, blimps, ornithopters, or something new. Out of scope: ready-to-fly kits you didn't design, weapons or payload drops, anything that carries a person, and high-power rockets.

## Can I use a kit?

Off-the-shelf parts like motors, ESCs and flight controllers are fine. The design has to be yours. A ready-to-fly kit you just assembled doesn't count.

## What if it crashes?

Rebuild it and log the repair. You need one good flight on video, not a perfect first try.

## How do I get the money?

Once your design is approved, the grant is sent as an HCB card for parts. You spend it like a normal card on the things in your BOM.

## Can I submit to another program too?

No. A project can only be funded once, so no double-dipping across Hack Club programs.
